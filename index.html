about

I'm a backend developer with over nine years of experience — most of it spent in the parts of the stack that users never see but always depend on.

Outside of work, I gravitate toward security, privacy, and self-hosting. I'd rather build my own solution than trust someone else's. I run a homelab, tinker with hardware and software for fun, and occasionally write about what I learn.

This site is intentionally minimal. Source on GitHub.
